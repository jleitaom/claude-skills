# Self-check quizzes

Optional (on by default). Short: 3–5 questions per guide section, 4–6 per project.

## Mix of question types
- **Explain it** — "In your own words, why does …?"
- **Predict** — "If you double X, what happens to Y?"
- **Spot the bug / pitfall** — a short snippet or scenario with something wrong.
- **Apply** — "Which method would you pick for …, and why?"
- For projects: questions about *their* results ("Why did the baseline win on …?").

## Format (Markdown, answers hidden)

```markdown
### Self-check

1. <question>
2. <question>

<details>
<summary>Answers</summary>

1. <answer, with the reason, and the § to reread if wrong>
2. …

</details>
```

If they paste their answers in chat, give feedback point by point and suggest
which section to revisit.
